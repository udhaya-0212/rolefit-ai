Graduate Data Analyst — Product Insights

We are looking for an early-career analyst to help product teams make evidence-based decisions.

Responsibilities
• Query and analyze large datasets using SQL and Python
• Build dashboards and communicate insights to stakeholders
• Develop forecasting or statistical models to support planning
• Collaborate with engineering and product partners
• Maintain reliable, well-documented analytical workflows

Requirements
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics or related field
• Strong Python and SQL skills; experience with Pandas
• Knowledge of machine learning, regression or forecasting
• Experience building dashboards using Tableau or Power BI
• Familiarity with A/B testing and experimental design
• Clear written communication and cross-functional teamwork
• Git experience and attention to data quality
